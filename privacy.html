Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

OVOBRIAN TECH respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how OVOBRIAN TECH TikTok Manager may collect, use, store and protect information when you use the service.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the service may process:

  • Basic TikTok profile information made available through authorized access.
  • TikTok account identifiers and profile details.
  • Video information available through authorized TikTok API permissions.
  • Authentication tokens needed to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

We aim to request only information necessary for the features you choose to use.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Connect your TikTok account after your authorization.
  • Display permitted profile and video information.
  • Support video management and authorized publishing features.
  • Maintain service security and reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

3. TikTok Authorization

Account access is requested through TikTok's official authorization process. You choose whether to grant the requested permissions. The service can access only information and functions made available by the permissions and TikTok APIs.

4. Storage and Security

Our planned architecture uses a secure server-side system for information needed to provide the service. Authentication tokens will be protected using appropriate security controls, including encryption and restricted access. We will not intentionally expose private credentials in public website code.

Security measures will be implemented before users' account information or tokens are stored by the production service. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate, host, maintain or secure the service. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

When you use TikTok features, information may be exchanged with TikTok as required to provide those features and under TikTok's own policies.

6. Data Retention

Information will be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs or comply with applicable legal obligations. Retention periods and deletion procedures will be applied by the production service.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may stop using the service and revoke its TikTok permissions through the relevant TikTok account settings. You may also request deletion of information associated with your use of the service by contacting us.

We will provide a process to review and handle deletion requests, subject to information we are legally required to retain.

8. Children's Privacy

The service is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. Users must meet the age requirements applicable to their use of TikTok and this service.

9. Third-Party Services

OVOBRIAN TECH integrates with TikTok, which operates independently and has its own privacy practices. Please review TikTok's privacy policy for information about its handling of your data. OVOBRIAN TECH is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the service develops or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
